About us

We are a student association at EETAC (UPC) that designs, calculates and builds high-power rockets.

What Hyperion Rockets is

Hyperion Rockets is a non-profit student association at the School of Telecommunications and Aerospace Engineering of Castelldefels (EETAC), part of the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya.

We came together to design, calculate, build and launch experimental rockets, and to learn engineering by doing it.

What we want to do

  • Make the association's first high-power flight, with the support of SpainRocketry, at the Alcolea de Cinca launch site.
  • Step up, rocket by rocket, towards increasingly complex projects.
  • Give every member real engineering experience: design, calculation, manufacturing, testing and documentation.
