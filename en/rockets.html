Our rockets

The rockets we design, calculate and build as an association.

Helios

Our first high-power rocket. It is 1.15 metres long and 7.9 centimetres (3 inches) in diameter, and will fly on a class H motor. We are designing and building it ourselves, part by part.

Alpha

A small rocket, 43 centimetres long and 2.6 centimetres in diameter, with four fins and a class C motor.
